## 5. [TASK Memory: Beyond Text-Only Memory with Activation Steering and Knowledge Editing against Memory Hallucination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34762)

**Authors:** Jinkun Zhao, Wenjun Wu  
**Categories:** cs.AI · **Published:** 2026-11-25 · **Relevance:** 9

TL;DR: TASK Memory adds a small “activation steering” signal and a lightweight knowledge‑edit layer to standard text notes, letting frozen LLMs decide when to trust external memory and when to rely on their own weights. This reduces memory‑ignoring hallucinations to <2% and cuts mis‑application of memories by more than 70%, while also handling fact revisions and boosting multi‑hop accuracy.  
Why it matters: It gives practical, low‑overhead control over external memory in large language models, dramatically lowering hallucinations that arise from conflicting parametric and retrieved knowledge.

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34762) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34762)
