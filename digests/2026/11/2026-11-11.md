## 1. [Chain of Physics: Supporting Fault Diagnosis with Engineering-Inspired Large Language Model Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14263)

**Authors:** Wenqi Zhou, Leonardo Baldo, Kai Goebel, Manuel Arias Chao  
**Categories:** cs.LG, eess.SP · **Published:** 2026-11-10 · **Relevance:** 13

TL;DR: Chain of Physics (CoP) is an LLM framework that uses a automatically generated Constrained Knowledge Graph of engineering documents to link failures, physical changes, and sensors, then retrieves relevant graph paths as in‑context examples for the LLM to reason about faults. It boosts diagnostic accuracy by 8–20 % over plain prompting and 12–18 % over standard RAG on industrial benchmarks.  
Why it matters: CoP shows that structuring domain knowledge into a graph and feeding it as context lets LLMs diagnose complex equipment more reliably, which can accelerate maintenance and reduce downtime.

## 6. [Don't Just Trust Your Memory: Evidence-Linked Memory Reduces Memory-Induced Degradation in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14320)

**Categories:** cs.AI, cs.MA · **Published:** 2026-11-10 · **Relevance:** 8

TL;DR: WikiTrace links each memory lesson to its original trajectory so an LLM agent can verify or correct the lesson before using it, reducing errors that arise from blindly following past experiences. Why it matters: By enabling agents to cross‑check lessons against their source data, WikiTrace improves reliability and performance of LLM agents that rely on external memory for learning.

## 7. [Tree-Ising: Repairing Language Models with Bit-Level Ising Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14210)

**Authors:** Hadi Salloum, Ammar Ali  
**Categories:** cs.LG · **Published:** 2026-11-10 · **Relevance:** 8

TL;DR: Tree-Ising edits a language model’s BF16 weights by flipping signs or shifting exponents, solving a sparse Ising (QUBO) optimization on a GPU. It fixes reasoning loops in Qwen3.5‑0.8B, boosting MATH‑500 from 7.9% to 54.6% with far fewer weight changes and no LLM tokens.  
Why it matters: It shows a practical, gradient‑free, low‑cost method to patch LLM behavior by directly optimizing weight bits, enabling rapid, targeted repairs without expensive fine‑tuning.

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14210) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14210)
