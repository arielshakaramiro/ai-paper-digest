## 1. [Depth Before Breadth: Reverse-to-Forward Curriculum with Advantage Decomposition for LLM Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24697)

**Authors:** Siyuan Du, Yuxin Liu, Meng Tang, Haoran Li +3 more  
**Categories:** cs.AI · **Published:** 2026-11-18 · **Relevance:** 10

TL;DR: The paper introduces a new RL training scheme for LLM agents that first teaches the agent to reach easier, intermediate states (depth) before tackling the full task (breadth). This “reverse-to-forward” curriculum, combined with separate advantage signals for reaching solvable states and finishing the task, dramatically improves success rates on long‑horizon benchmarks.  
Why it matters: It offers a practical way to train large language models on complex, sparse‑reward tasks, boosting performance while reducing exploration difficulty.
