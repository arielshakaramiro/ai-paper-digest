## 2. [Learning to Think with Images Faithfully via Adaptive Data Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26199)

**Categories:** cs.CV, cs.CL · **Published:** 2026-11-19 · **Relevance:** 11

TL;DR: The paper shows that vision‑language models that actively zoom into images can become unfaithful by ignoring the zoomed evidence, but by selectively training on questions where zooming truly helps and rewarding tool‑free answers when it doesn’t, the models become both more accurate and more trustworthy.  
Why it matters: It demonstrates a practical way to improve the reliability of image‑based reasoning without sacrificing performance, which is crucial for deploying VLMs in safety‑critical or explainable AI systems.
