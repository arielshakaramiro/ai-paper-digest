## 2. [Terminal-style Mathematical Modeling Bench: The First Agentic and Verifiable Benchmark for Mathematical Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08237)

**Authors:** Xiaojun Wu, Cehao Yang, Xuhui Jiang, Chengjin Xu +2 more  
**Categories:** cs.CL · **Published:** 2026-11-05 · **Relevance:** 11

TL;DR: TB‑MathModel is a new benchmark that lets AI agents solve full real‑world math‑modeling contests in a terminal, returning a single JSON that is automatically verified against the official solution and a set of accepted papers. Why it matters: It provides a reproducible, machine‑checkable way to evaluate long‑horizon modeling skills and collect agent decision traces, enabling more reliable progress tracking for AI‑driven scientific problem solving.
