## 4. [Same Outputs, Different Scores: Auditing the Unchecked Graders of Multimodal LLM Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03377)

**Authors:** Ahmad Jarjis Hasan, Jonathan Lwowski  
**Categories:** cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.CV · **Published:** 2026-11-02 · **Relevance:** 9

TL;DR: Benchmark scoring rules for multimodal LLMs are often arbitrary and can shift reported accuracy by up to 22 points, even when the model’s actual answer is unchanged. The authors show that different scoring strategies (fallbacks, regex matching, likelihood) can either inflate or deflate scores, making it hard to tell if a model truly understands the task.  
Why it matters: Accurate, transparent scoring is essential for reliably comparing multimodal LLMs and for understanding their real capabilities.
