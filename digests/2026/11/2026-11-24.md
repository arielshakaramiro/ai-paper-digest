## 3. [CO-GRAFT: Attributing Reinforcement Learning Gains in Search Agents via Role-Level Policy Grafting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31185)

**Authors:** Ramraj Chandradevan, Sayontan Ghosh, Vinoth Selvendran  
**Categories:** cs.IR, cs.AI, cs.CL · **Published:** 2026-11-23 · **Relevance:** 9

TL;DR: CO‑GRAFT lets you split an RL‑trained search agent into four role‑specific sub‑policies (reasoning, decision, query, answer) and, without retraining, compute Shapley values to see how much each role contributes to the overall performance boost. The study finds that most of the RL gains come from better answer extraction and search retention, not from generating better queries, and that the importance of each role shifts with model size and initialization.  
Why it matters: It gives engineers a practical, weight‑agnostic tool to pinpoint which parts of a search agent are truly improving after RL, enabling more targeted fine‑tuning and resource allocation.

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31185) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.31185)

## 6. [Language Models Steer on Injected Uncertainty Tokens, but Fail to Self-Monitor In Situ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31228)

**Authors:** Daniel Theron  
**Categories:** cs.AI · **Published:** 2026-11-23 · **Relevance:** 7

TL;DR: Language models can be nudged to correct mistakes when told to doubt, but they don’t notice their own errors as they generate. Their internal confidence signals stay high even when they’re wrong, so they rarely backtrack on their own.  
Why it matters: This shows that self‑monitoring is the real bottleneck for reliable, agentic reasoning in current open‑weight models.

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31228) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.31228)

## 7. [Decoupling Solving from Abstention: Single-Rollout Conformal Risk Control for Hard-Budget LLM Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31065)

**Authors:** Qianyi Chen, Bo Li  
**Categories:** cs.AI, stat.ME · **Published:** 2026-11-23 · **Relevance:** 7

TL;DR: Tail‑CRC lets a large language model finish reasoning within a hard token budget while still knowing when to give up, using only one forward pass per question.
