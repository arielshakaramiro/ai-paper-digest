## 4. [SOTA: Stock Options Trading Agents Guided by Option-Implied Return Distributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10407)

**Authors:** Yizhen Xie, Mengyang Liu  
**Categories:** cs.AI, cs.LG, q-fin.PM, q-fin.TR · **Published:** 2026-10-07 · **Relevance:** 8

TL;DR: SOTA trains a large language model to pick option‑strategy bundles (like straddles, spreads, etc.) instead of individual contracts, then uses a deterministic resolver to build the actual portfolio. Why it matters: It shows that a language‑model‑guided agent can flexibly adapt strategy choices to market conditions and deliver superior risk‑adjusted returns in real option markets.

## 10. [Agentic RSR: Real-to-Sim-to-Real through Scene Reconstruction and Execution-Grounded Robot Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10479)
