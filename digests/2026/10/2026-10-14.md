## 1. [SEAR: Self-Evolving Agentic Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18792)

**Authors:** Xinyi Wang, Bangzheng Li, Hanyu Wang, Lin Zhang +4 more  
**Categories:** cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CL · **Published:** 2026-10-13 · **Relevance:** 16

TL;DR: Language‑model agents can autonomously write, test, and keep control code for robots, improving performance over time. ## 2. [ChunkAct: Action Chunking for Agentic Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18874)

**Authors:** Bingxin Xu, Wenrui Bao, Xinxin Liu, Jiawei Zhou +2 more  
**Categories:** cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.CV · **Published:** 2026-10-13 · **Relevance:** 14

Why it matters: It makes large‑language‑model‑driven robots faster and cheaper while keeping or improving task success.

## 6. [Back-Translation Still Matters: Leveraging Monolingual Data for Low-Resource Machine Translation with LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18818)

**Authors:** Divyanshu Bathla, Parag Singla, Jitin Singla  
**Categories:** cs.CL, cs.AI · **Published:** 2026-10-13 · **Relevance:** 9

TL;DR: Back‑translation (BT) of monolingual data with a fine‑tuned LLM dramatically improves low‑resource translation, outperforming both continued pre‑training and models trained on far more parallel data. Even with only 1,000 parallel pairs, BT boosts COMET‑XXL scores by 7.7–16.4× in domain and by 16–173% over parallel‑only fine‑tuning across five low‑resource languages.  
Why it matters: BT turns readily available monolingual corpora into high‑quality synthetic parallel data, enabling practical, cost‑effective translation systems for languages with scarce resources.

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18818) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18818)

## 7. [RSIgen: Reciprocal Self-Improvement for Agentic Visual Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18688)

**Authors:** Yidi Liu, Haozhe Wang, Jie Xiao, Kai Zhu +2 more  
**Categories:** cs.CV · **Published:** 2026-10-13 · **Relevance:** 9

TL;DR: RSIgen lets a visual generation model and its internal agent teach each other by dynamically choosing who learns, refreshing training signals, and building a curriculum that adapts over cycles. Why it matters: It provides a practical, cycle‑based method to keep both generation and decision‑making components of multimodal models up‑to‑date, leading to better real‑world outcomes.
