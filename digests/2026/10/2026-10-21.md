## 2. [Capable Teacher, Myopic Scorer: Reward-Free Teacher Repair for Agentic On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28586)

**Authors:** Shu Zhao, Tan Yu  
**Categories:** cs.CL, cs.AI · **Published:** 2026-10-20 · **Relevance:** 10

TL;DR: The paper identifies that agentic on‑policy distillation fails because teachers score tokens without considering the episode’s final outcome, letting safe but suboptimal moves slip through. It introduces Rollout‑to‑Scorer Distillation (R2S), which repairs the teacher’s scoring by using teacher takeovers on high‑disagreement states, then fine‑tunes the teacher to give outcome‑aware scores—achieving better student performance without any task reward.  
Why it matters: R2S provides a reward‑free, practical method to improve on‑policy distillation, enabling more reliable student agents in complex environments.

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28586) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28586)
