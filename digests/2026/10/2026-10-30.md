## 5. [Locating and Closing Information Gaps in Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40186)

**Authors:** Yaxin Gong, Chongming Gao, Gangyi Zhang, Chenxiao Fan +6 more  
**Categories:** cs.AI · **Published:** 2026-10-29 · **Relevance:** 10

TL;DR: SPLICE automatically finds missing information in a fixed multi‑agent workflow, adds a single directed link to deliver the needed evidence, re‑runs the affected agents, and then chooses whether to keep the new answer or stick with the old one. It boosts web‑tool‑use accuracy by 8 % and reasoning benchmarks by 5.7 % with only a modest increase in cost.  
Why it matters: It lets existing LLM‑based multi‑agent pipelines self‑repair information gaps without redesigning the whole workflow.

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40186) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40186)
