## 7. [Same Simulation, Different Evidence: Where Retrieved Tool Output Costs LLM Agents Accuracy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26836)

**Authors:** Ke Zhang, Sahchit Chundur, Jerry Peprah Owusu, Mohammad Javad Abdolhosseini Qomi +1 more  
**Categories:** cs.AI · **Published:** 2026-10-19 · **Relevance:** 7

TL;DR: When LLM agents keep large tool outputs out of the prompt and retrieve them later, GPT‑5 models lose about 10 % accuracy on a geochemistry simulation task, while Claude models are unaffected. The loss comes from the agents substituting the retrieved evidence with their own higher‑precision outputs and mis‑rounding, rather than from failing to run the simulation.  
Why it matters: The study shows that how an agent manages tool‑output context can materially affect its correctness, so engineers must evaluate context‑management strategies for accuracy, not just overall performance.

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26836) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.26836)
