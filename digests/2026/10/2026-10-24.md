## 2. [Certified Execution Harness for Agentic AI: Safe and Least-Intrusive Petri-Net Supervisory Control Between Reasoning and Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31376)

**Authors:** Chao Gu, Quanyan Zhu  
**Categories:** cs.AI, eess.SY · **Published:** 2026-10-22 · **Relevance:** 13

TL;DR: The paper builds a Petri‑net based safety controller that blocks unsafe tool calls from LLM agents, even if attackers try to trick the model. Why it matters: It gives a principled, formally verified way to keep LLM agents from executing dangerous or over‑budgeted actions, which is critical for deploying them safely in production.
