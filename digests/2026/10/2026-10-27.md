## 4. [Evolve on Graph: Self-Evolving Knowledge Graph Reasoning without Question Annotations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35598)

**Authors:** Zongyuan Sun, Shuai Zhao, Ke Shu, Ziyue Zhu +7 more  
**Categories:** cs.AI · **Published:** 2026-10-26 · **Relevance:** 9

TL;DR: Evolve on Graph (EoG) lets a large language model improve its multi‑hop reasoning on knowledge graphs without any question‑answer pairs by playing a self‑generated game: it proposes questions, answers them, and uses logic queries to verify the answers, gradually raising the difficulty as it gets better. Why it matters: It enables practical, annotation‑free enhancement of LLM reasoning on industry‑scale knowledge graphs, reducing the need for costly human labeling or proprietary training data.

## 9. [Free to Look, Free to Reason: Simple LLM Harnesses Outdiagnose Hand-Crafted Root Cause Analysis Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35767)

**Authors:** Tingzhu Bi, Xun Zhang, Jinglin Li, Ping Wang +1 more  
**Categories:** cs.AI, cs.DC, cs.SE · **Published:** 2026-10-26 · **Relevance:** 7

TL;DR: Simple LLM harnesses that let the model freely reason—using one evidence call, a SQL agent, or a coding agent—outperform complex, hand‑crafted RCA agents and traditional methods on a benchmark of 90 microservice failure cases. They achieve 65–77% precision@1 on mid‑tier models, versus 16–53% for published agents and 6–39% for non‑LLM baselines.  
Why it matters: Engineers can replace elaborate, pipeline‑based RCA systems with a single, flexible LLM harness, gaining higher accuracy and lower cost without sacrificing reliability.
