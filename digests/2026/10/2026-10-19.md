## 1. [SkillBrowser: Selective Skill Exposure for Scalable Skill-Using Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23554)

**Authors:** Jin Ye, Yifan Jiang, Lan-zhe Guo  
**Categories:** cs.AI · **Published:** 2026-10-16 · **Relevance:** 14

TL;DR: SkillBrowser lets large‑language‑model agents load only the skills they actually need, improving accuracy on complex tasks while avoiding the confusion that comes from seeing too many skills at once. It does this by having the agent identify its current skill gaps, ask a targeted query, and pull in just the right tools on demand.  
Why it matters: By reducing unnecessary skill exposure, SkillBrowser makes it practical to scale up skill libraries for real‑world LLM agents without hurting performance or adding excessive latency.

[Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23554) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23554)
